Chemistry lens: Organic, Inorganic, Physical, Analytical, Biochemistry, and Materials are mutually exclusive primary fields. QA default: protocol_correct / planned. Incorrect, format-invalid, and length-stopped responses remain in the planned denominator. Item filters change only the evidence list below, not the aggregate denominator above.

content_match is the existing extractor's content match, not a human semantic review. Conditional accuracy uses only protocol-valid responses as its denominator and does not replace the default score.

oMeBench: exact mechanism match, S_total, and S_partial are reported separately. Partial alignment with the reference mechanism does not establish chemical feasibility.

Atom-index control: the statistical unit is ten molecules. DeepSeek's 30 planned cells were not executed and remain N/A; historical QA answers are not substituted.

Crystal: direct parsing excludes the author's upstream postprocessing but retains the existing parser tolerances. Author-postprocessed results are shown separately. Candidate, structure @1, and structure @2 views use different denominators. No energy, stability, or synthesizability metric is available.
